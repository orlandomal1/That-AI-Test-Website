# That AI — Brand Guidelines (C03 Human-System Bridge)

## Logo
- **File:** `logo.png` — cyan short lockup (symbol + "That AI" wordmark)
- **Dark surfaces:** White arcs + cyan accent. Never on cyan background.
- **Light surfaces:** All-black version only. No cyan on white bg.
- **Website header:** Symbol 28px left, wordmark right, DM Sans Bold 14px. Min clearspace = 1× symbol height.

## Colour Palette
| Name          | Hex       |
|---------------|-----------|
| System Black  | `#0A0A0A` |
| Void Grey     | `#1A1A1A` |
| Bridge Cyan   | `#00E5FF` |
| Depth Cyan    | `#00B8CC` |
| Signal White  | `#CCCCCC` |
| Mid Signal    | `#888888` |

## Typography
| Role              | Font                          | Notes                              |
|-------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Display/Headline  | DM Sans ExtraBold             | Hero statements only               |
| Wordmark          | DM Sans Bold                  | Wide tracking (+80), sentence case |
| Body / UI         | Inter Regular/Medium          | 8.5–10pt, leading 1.5×             |
| Mono/Code/Labels  | IBM Plex Mono                 | URLs, specs, section heads         |

## Brand Essence
> "That AI bridges the gap between what a business is and what it could become with intelligent automation. We do not replace people — we amplify them."

## Primary Message
**"Intelligence, bridged."**

Secondary: "We build the intelligence layer between your team and your tools."

## Tone of Voice
- Direct, never blunt
- Intelligent, never academic
- Confident, never arrogant
- Human, never casual
- Precise, never cold

## Symbol Logic (C03)
- Two opposing semicircular arcs — left (human, white/black), right (system, cyan)
- Single horizontal connector bar with cyan terminal dots
- Cyan appears on right arc + terminal dots only — technology responds to human, not reverse
