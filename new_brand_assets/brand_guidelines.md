# That AI — Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Essence

> "That AI bridges the gap between what a business is and what it could become with intelligent automation. We do not replace people — we amplify them."

We build the intelligence layer between your team and your tools.

**Primary message:** *Intelligence, bridged.*

---

## 2. The Logo

### 2.1 Concept

The symbol is built from three structural elements:

- **A white arc on the left** — the human side. Open, receiving, contextual.
- **A cyan arc on the right** — the system side. Precise, responsive, definite.
- **A horizontal cyan connector with two terminal dots** — the bridge between them. The connector passes through the point where the two arcs meet.

The asymmetric colour is intentional. Cyan appears only on the system-side arc, the connector bar, and the terminal dots. Technology responds to the human, not the reverse.

### 2.2 Geometry Specification

- **Arc radius:** 54 units (reference scale). Each arc is a true semicircle.
- **Stroke weight:** 8 units at reference scale (≈3.7% of total symbol height).
- **Connector bar:** 6 units stroke weight, length 92 units (from x = −46 to x = +46 at reference scale).
- **Terminal dots:** 7 unit radius, sat at the connector bar's endpoints.
- **Touch point:** the two arcs meet at the symbol's geometric centre. The connector bar passes through this point.
- **Total symbol height at reference scale:** 108 units.

### 2.3 Lockups

Three official lockups:

1. **Symbol only** — used at favicon scale, app icon, social avatar, and any context where the wordmark would compete with the surrounding UI.
2. **Short lockup** — symbol + "That AI" wordmark to the right.
3. **Long lockup** — symbol + "That AI Consultancy" wordmark to the right. For legal and registration contexts only.

The short lockup is the primary brand identifier. The long lockup is a variant.

### 2.4 Lockup Proportions (Critical)

- The **symbol** is rendered **50% taller** than the wordmark cap height.
- The **wordmark cap height** is matched to a font size where cap height = 108 reference units. The symbol then renders at 162 reference units total height.
- The **vertical centres** of the symbol and the wordmark must align on the same horizontal axis. The wordmark's *optical centre* (cap-height midpoint) sits on this axis, not the baseline.
- **Clearspace:** a horizontal gap of one full symbol radius (54 reference units) between the symbol's rightmost edge and the wordmark's leftmost glyph.
- **Padding:** minimum clearspace on all sides equal to the connector's terminal-dot radius (7 reference units).

### 2.5 Colour Treatments (Three Approved Variants)

| Set | Surface | Left arc | Right arc | Bar + dots | Wordmark |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Dark surface (primary)** | `#0A0A0A` | `#FFFFFF` | `#00E5FF` | `#00E5FF` | `#FFFFFF` |
| **2 — Light surface, black wordmark** | `#FFFFFF` | `#0A0A0A` | `#00E5FF` | `#00E5FF` | `#0A0A0A` |
| **3 — Light surface, cyan wordmark** | `#FFFFFF` | `#0A0A0A` | `#00E5FF` | `#00E5FF` | `#00E5FF` |

Set 1 is the primary brand expression and should be the default in any digital interface, deck cover, or hero context. Sets 2 and 3 exist for light-surface applications (print, document headers, embossed materials, light-themed UI).

The cyan always appears on the right arc, the connector, and the terminal dots — regardless of surface or wordmark colour. This rule is non-negotiable.

## 4. Typography

| Role | Font | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display / Headline | **DM Sans ExtraBold** | Hero statements only |
| Wordmark | **DM Sans Bold** | Wide tracking (+80), sentence case, never uppercase |
| Body / UI | **Inter Regular / Medium** | 8.5–10pt, leading 1.5× |
| Mono / Code / Labels | **IBM Plex Mono** | URLs, specs, section headers, small caps |

### 4.1 Wordmark Typography in Lockups

- Set in **DM Sans Bold** at a size where cap height equals 108 reference units (= ⅔ of the symbol's drawn height).
- Letter-spacing: −2 (slightly tight).
- Always sentence case: "That AI" and "That AI Consultancy" — never uppercase, never small caps.
- No italic, no oblique, no condensed variant.

## 5. Tone of Voice

- **Direct**, never blunt.
- **Intelligent**, never academic.
- **Confident**, never arrogant.
- **Human**, never casual.
- **Precise**, never cold.

We are a consultancy that happens to specialise in intelligent automation, not an AI startup. The voice reflects that: structured, deliberate, written rather than typed.

---

## 6. Symbol Logic Summary

- Two opposing semicircular arcs of equal stroke weight: human (white/black) on the left, system (cyan) on the right.
- They are equal in weight because we believe in equal partnership between people and the systems we build.
- The bulge tips of both arcs meet at the symbol's geometric centre, creating one shared touch point.
- A horizontal cyan connector with terminal dots passes through this touch point, making the bridge visible.
- The asymmetric cyan application (right arc + bar + dots only) signals that technology responds to the human, not the other way around.

---

## 7. Usage Rules

**Do:**

- Use the short lockup ("That AI") in 95% of contexts.
- Reserve the long lockup ("That AI Consultancy") for legal documents, registration, and contexts requiring the full registered entity name.
- Maintain the symbol-to-wordmark proportion: symbol 50% taller than wordmark cap height, vertically centred on the wordmark's optical centre.
- Use Set 1 (dark surface) as the default for all digital and presentation contexts.

**Do not:**

- Re-colour the cyan elements — Bridge Cyan (`#00E5FF`) is fixed.
- Place the symbol on a cyan background — contrast is insufficient.
- Stretch, rotate, or distort the symbol.
- Replace either arc with a different shape, or close the arcs into full circles.
- Add additional terminal dots or extend the connector beyond its specified length.
- Use the cyan wordmark variant (Set 3) on dark surfaces — it lacks contrast.
